# Code of conduct

Playkiln is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing concrete behavior, respect differing experience levels, and keep criticism focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner until the Playkiln support address is operational. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
